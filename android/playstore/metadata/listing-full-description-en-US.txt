Bring black-and-white photos back to life with ChromaSnap.

ChromaSnap uses AI to colorize vintage images while preserving subject identity and composition. You can then upscale, sharpen, and enhance vibrancy for a cleaner final result. For selected images, generate a short animation to make memories feel alive again.

Key features:
- AI colorization for black-and-white photos
- One-tap enhancement tools: upscale, sharpen, vibrancy
- Optional image animation workflow
- Account-based token usage and subscription tiers
- Google Play Billing integration for Android purchases
- Secure account authentication and recovery
- In-app account deletion support

Who it is for:
- Families restoring personal photo archives
- Creators making social-ready historical content
- Anyone who wants fast, high-quality AI restoration from mobile

How it works:
1. Upload a black-and-white image.
2. Choose a style/palette.
3. Generate colorized output.
4. Enhance quality or animate.
5. Save and share.

Billing and subscriptions:
- Subscriptions and token top-ups are managed through Google Play Billing on Android.
- Token balance and usage are visible in-app.

Support:
- For support, use the contact details listed in the Play Store listing.
- Privacy Policy and account management links should be provided in Play Console before rollout.
